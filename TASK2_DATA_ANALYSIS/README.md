# Task 2 – Data Analysis

## Objective

The objective of this task is to perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) on a sales dataset using Python. The analysis focuses on understanding the dataset, calculating descriptive statistics, identifying trends and relationships, detecting outliers, and answering important business questions.

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains information about customer purchases and sales transactions.

The analysis includes variables such as:

- Customer information
- Gender
- Product category
- Purchase amount
- Payment method
- Transaction details

## Analysis Performed

The following analysis was performed using Python and Pandas:

- Loaded and examined the dataset
- Checked dataset structure and features
- Calculated descriptive statistics
- Analyzed numerical and categorical variables
- Studied distributions and trends
- Analyzed relationships between variables
- Identified high-value outlier transactions
- Answered important business questions

## Key Insights

- Electronics generated the highest revenue among all product categories.
- Beauty had the highest number of purchases.
- Credit Card was the most frequently used payment method.
- Male customers were higher in number than female customers.
- The average purchase value was ₹17,331.01.
- A total of **16 high-value outlier transactions** were identified.
- Although Beauty had the highest number of purchases, Electronics generated much higher revenue. This indicates that Electronics transactions generally had higher purchase values.

## Outlier Analysis

Outlier analysis was performed to identify unusually high-value purchase transactions.

A total of **16 high-value outlier transactions** were identified.

These transactions can have a significant impact on the overall revenue and average purchase value.

## Conclusion

The exploratory data analysis provided useful insights into customer purchasing behavior and sales performance.

Electronics was the strongest category in terms of revenue, while Beauty had the highest number of purchases. Credit Card was the most popular payment method, and male customers represented the larger customer group.

The identification of high-value outliers also helped understand unusual purchasing patterns that may influence business decisions.

Overall, the analysis demonstrates how data analysis can be used to understand sales trends, customer behavior, and business performance.
